# Go-to-market playbook — how to sell CRAZY BUSINESS APP

A practical plan for winning your first 100 paying shops in India, then scaling.
Prices, numbers and timelines are **starting assumptions** — measure and adjust.

---

## 1. Who to sell to first (pick a beachhead)

Don't sell to "all SMBs". Start with one segment where the product is clearly
better and where shop owners talk to each other:

**Beachhead: CCTV, computer, IT and electronics dealers (1–10 staff).**

Why them:
- They need what generic billing apps do badly: **serial numbers, warranty lookup,
  service tickets, AMC renewals, quotations that convert to invoices**, and stock
  across a godown + shop. This app has all of that.
- They cluster in markets (Lamington Road Mumbai, Nehru Place Delhi, SP Road
  Bengaluru, Ritchie Street Chennai, CTC Hyderabad, Chandni Chowk electronics lanes,
  plus every district town's "computer market") — **one good demo spreads to the
  whole lane.**
- They buy from a handful of distributors who can push you to hundreds of dealers.

Second wave (month 4+): mobile accessory shops, electrical/hardware traders,
small distributors, service companies (AC/RO/solar installers with AMCs), freelancers.

**Ideal customer profile (ICP) checklist** — qualify every lead:
- [ ] GST-registered (or about to be), issues 50+ invoices a month
- [ ] Sells items with serial numbers / warranty, or does service/AMC work
- [ ] Today uses paper bills, Excel, or a desktop billing tool they find painful
- [ ] Owner uses WhatsApp for business and an Android phone
- [ ] Has had a "customer owes me money and I forgot" problem (everyone has)

## 2. Positioning — what to say in one breath

> **"Bill, track stock with serial numbers, and collect your dues on WhatsApp —
> from your phone. One App. Complete Business Control."**

Three pains → three promises:

| Pain the owner feels | What you promise | Proof in the demo |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "Paise atke hue hain" — money stuck with customers | Know who owes what, send a WhatsApp reminder in 2 taps | Outstanding screen → ageing → WhatsApp reminder |
| "Which serial did I sell to whom? Warranty kab tak hai?" | Scan a serial, see the customer, invoice and warranty | Barcode scan → product with serial → invoice |
| "Profit kitna hua, pata hi nahi chalta" | Sales, cash received and profit shown separately | Dashboard: Sales vs Cash collected vs Profit estimate |

**Differentiators to lean on** (all real, all in the product):
1. Serial-number & warranty tracking + service tickets + AMC in the same app.
2. Quotation → sales order → invoice → payment in one flow; GST worked out automatically (CGST/SGST or IGST from the place of supply).
3. Staff logins with roles: salesperson can't see your cost price or profit; big discounts need your approval.
4. Honest numbers: sales, cash and profit kept apart (most owners confuse these).
5. Phone-first, works on weak networks: invoices save offline and sync later.
6. Your data is yours: full export any time, on every plan.

**Never claim** (not built — will destroy trust and may be illegal):
- ❌ "GST return filing", "e-invoice / IRN", "e-way bill", "GST portal integration"
- ❌ "Automatic WhatsApp messages" (it opens WhatsApp with the message; the owner presses send)
- ❌ "100% accurate profit" (it's an estimate from recorded costs)
- ❌ Fake reviews, fake user counts, or "No. 1 app" claims

Say instead: "GST-ready invoices your CA can use", "WhatsApp reminder in 2 taps".

**Competitors** you will hear about: Vyapar, myBillBook, Khatabook, Zoho Invoice/Books,
Tally, Marg, Busy, and local desktop software. Don't bash them. Ask: *"Is it tracking
your serial numbers, warranty and AMC renewals? Can your salesman see your cost
price?"* — then show it. Check their current prices before quoting comparisons.

## 3. Pricing strategy (how to use the plans)

Plans: Free / Starter ₹299 / Pro ₹699 / Business ₹1,499 per month (see [PRICING.md](PRICING.md)).

- **Lead with Pro.** Every new business gets a 14-day Pro trial — demo Pro features
  (serials, service, staff, purchases). After the trial they keep Free, so nobody loses data.
- **Anchor on daily cost:** "₹699 a month is ₹23 a day — less than one chai and samosa —
  and it recovers money you're losing to forgotten dues."
- **Sell yearly:** ₹6,990/year = 2 months free. Offer yearly at the moment of the
  first "aha" (first reminder that brought money back).
- **Founding-customer offer** (first 100 shops): price locked for life + free setup
  (you import their products and customers from Excel). Creates urgency honestly.
- **Never discount the monthly price silently.** Give extra value (setup, training,
  an extra month) instead — protects your price for referrals.
- Register for GST and add 18% GST on your subscription invoices; state clearly
  whether prices include GST.

## 4. Channels — in order of priority

### Channel 1: Field sales in markets (months 1–6, highest conversion)
- One person, one market, every day. Walk shop to shop at **11 am–1 pm and 4–6 pm**
  (avoid opening rush and evening peak).
- Goal per day: 25 shop visits → 8 conversations → 3 demos → 1 sign-up on the spot.
- **Set up their business on their phone during the visit** (business name, GSTIN,
  5 products, 3 customers, 1 real invoice shared on WhatsApp). An account with a real
  invoice in it converts 5–10× better than "I'll try later".
- Follow up on day 2 (WhatsApp), day 5 (call), day 12 (visit before trial ends).
- Pay field reps a base + per paid activation (e.g. one month of the plan as bonus,
  paid only after the 2nd month's payment to prevent fake sign-ups).

### Channel 2: Distributors (scale lever)
- CCTV/IT distributors have 200–2,000 dealers each. Offer them:
  - a **revenue share** (e.g. 20% of the first year) for dealers they bring, or
  - bulk licences they bundle with stock purchases ("Buy ₹2 lakh of stock, get 1 year Pro free").
- Give them a co-branded WhatsApp brochure and a 2-minute demo video.
- Sponsor their dealer meets: 10-minute live demo, sign-up desk at the back.

### Channel 3: Chartered accountants & tax practitioners
- Every CA handles 50–300 small businesses who send bills in messy formats.
- Pitch: "Your clients' sales, purchases, GST summary and outstanding in clean
  CSV/PDF — fewer WhatsApp photos of bills."
- Offer CAs a partner programme: free account, referral commission (e.g. 20% for 12
  months), and priority support for their clients.
- Visit CA offices in the same districts as your shop clusters.

### Channel 4: WhatsApp-first marketing (cheap, works in India)
- WhatsApp Business account with catalogue, quick replies, and labels (Lead, Demo done, Trial, Paid).
- **Only message people who opted in** (they gave you their number for this). Use
  broadcast lists (they only receive if they saved your number). For bulk/automated
  messages use the official WhatsApp Business Platform with approved templates —
  never unofficial bulk-sender tools (numbers get banned).
- Content that gets forwarded: 30-second screen recordings in Hindi/local language:
  "Udhaar wapas kaise laayen", "Serial number se warranty check", "GST bill 30 second mein".

### Channel 5: YouTube & Instagram Reels (compounding)
- Hindi + one regional language (Tamil/Telugu/Marathi/Kannada by your first market).
- Weekly: 1 long tutorial (5–8 min) + 3 Reels/Shorts (≤45 s).
- Video ideas: "CCTV dealer ka poora business ek app mein", "Quotation se invoice
  1 click mein", "Salesman ko cost price kaise chhupaye", customer story videos (with
  written permission).
- Every video: link to sign-up with a UTM tag; pin a comment with the founding offer.

### Channel 6: Google Play & Search (intent-driven)
- Play Store listing (ASO): title with keywords "GST Billing, Invoice & Stock"; first
  line of description states the 3 promises; screenshots with Hindi captions.
- Ask happy customers for honest reviews in person (never pay for or fake reviews).
- Google Ads (small budget, ₹500–1,000/day to start): exact/phrase match on
  "gst billing software for computer shop", "cctv billing software", "serial number
  inventory software", "amc management software". Send to a landing page per segment.
- SEO blog: "GST invoice format for CCTV installation", "How to track AMC renewals",
  "HSN codes for computer accessories" — useful pages that rank and convert.

### Channel 7: Referrals (from month 2)
- "Give 1 month, get 1 month": the referrer gets a free month when the referred shop
  pays for its first month.
- Ask right after a win: the moment a reminder brings money back, ask "Who else in
  this market should use this?"

## 5. The 7-minute demo script

1. **(30 s) Hook:** "How much money is stuck with customers right now?" Let them answer.
2. **(1 min) Outstanding:** show the dues list with ageing → tap WhatsApp reminder →
   message ready with amount and your UPI ID. "Two taps. Daily."
3. **(2 min) Quote → invoice:** make a quotation for a 4-camera kit for a customer in
   another state → IGST appears automatically → convert to invoice → share PDF on WhatsApp.
4. **(1 min) Serial & warranty:** scan a serial → see which customer, which invoice,
   warranty end date.
5. **(1 min) Staff control:** log in as salesperson → cost price and profit hidden;
   big discount → waits for the owner's approval (approve from the web app, on phone or laptop).
6. **(1 min) Dashboard:** today's sales, cash actually received, profit estimate — "three
   different numbers, most apps mix them up."
7. **(30 s) Close:** "Let's set up your shop now — 5 minutes, your 14-day trial starts
   today, and the founding price is locked for life if you join this month."

## 7. Activation & retention (where revenue is actually won)

Define **activation** = within 7 days the shop has issued **3 invoices** and
**recorded 1 payment**. Track it weekly; it predicts paying better than sign-ups.

Onboarding sequence (WhatsApp + in-app):
- Day 0: setup call or visit; import products/customers from their Excel (free).
- Day 1: "Send your first WhatsApp reminder" nudge.
- Day 3: tip video for their segment (serials / AMC).
- Day 7: check-in call — anything blocking?
- Day 12: "Trial ends in 2 days" + yearly offer.
- Day 15: Free-plan users: show what they'd get back on Pro (serials, staff logins).

Retention habits to build: daily dashboard check, weekly dues reminders, monthly
export for the CA. Churn early-warning: no invoice for 7 days → call them.

## 10. Marketing compliance in India (don't skip)

- **DPDP Act 2023:** collect only needed data, get clear consent for marketing
  messages, honour opt-outs and deletion requests quickly. Have a lawyer finalise the
  privacy policy before launch.
- **WhatsApp:** message only opted-in contacts; use approved templates on the official
  Business Platform for bulk/automated sends.
- **SMS:** promotional SMS requires TRAI DLT registration (entity + templates).
- **Advertising:** follow ASCI guidelines — no misleading claims, no fake testimonials,
  disclose paid influencer promotions (#ad).
- **Customer stories:** written permission before using a shop's name, logo or numbers;
  never show real customers' data in demos — use the fictional demo business.
- **Trademark:** search "CRAZY BUSINESS" on the IP India trademark registry and file
  in classes 9 and 42 before spending on brand marketing.
